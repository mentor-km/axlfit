Our Story
WE BUILT
THE GYM WE
WANTED.

Started in 2019 by two coaches tired of gyms that prioritised aesthetics over athletes. AXL Fit was built from the floor up — every decision made by people who actually train.

How It Started
FROM ONE
GARAGE TO
12,000 MEMBERS

In 2019, Vikram Desai and Kavya Nair opened AXL Fit with 800 sq ft, two power racks, and a waiting list from day one. Word spread fast — because results did.

By 2022 we expanded to our current 15,000 sq ft flagship. No investors, no franchise — just reinvested revenue and a community that kept showing up.

Today AXL Fit is home to 12,000+ active members, 48 coaches, and a recovery zone that rivals the best wellness centres in the country.
