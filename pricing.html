Is there a joining fee?

No joining fee this month. After the promotion ends, a one-time ₹499 joining fee applies. Lock in now.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes. Pro and Elite members can pause up to 30 days per year — useful for travel or injury. Starter members get 15 days.

What happens after the free trial?

We'll reach out before day 7. If you want to continue, your billing starts then. No auto-charge without your confirmation.

Are classes included in Starter?

Starter gets 5 group classes per month. Additional classes can be booked at ₹150 per session.
